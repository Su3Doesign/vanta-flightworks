01 · The brief

Helmets that see further than the pilot.

Vanta builds helmet-mounted display systems for test pilots and aerobatic teams. Each VF-9 shell is laid up by hand, scanned to the wearer's head to within 0.3 mm, and fitted with a visor display that puts airspeed, altitude and g-load in the line of sight.

HUD
02 · The visor

Symbology at infinity.

A binocular projection on the inner face of the visor, collimated so the symbols sit on the horizon and the eye never refocuses. 2,500 nits, readable against direct sun at altitude.

Shell mass1.38 kg

With the display module fitted. Lighter than most standard-issue shells.

Sustained load9 G

Neck load balanced so the centre of gravity sits over the spine at full pull.

Display field40° × 30°

Binocular, full colour, with the pilot's own eyes tracked at 240 Hz.
